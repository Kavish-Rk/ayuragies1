THE PROBLEM

Early knee OA is hard to catch — especially far from a hospital.

Osteoarthritis of the knee develops silently. By the time pain limits daily life, options narrow. Conventional assessment depends on specialist examination and imaging — not easily available in remote or underserved settings.

◉

Silent progression

Early cartilage changes show subtle signs that are easy to miss without targeted screening.

◎

Access gap

Specialist clinics and imaging are concentrated in cities — difficult to take to villages and camps.

⬢

Need for screening aid

A non-invasive, portable, offline-capable screening aid can flag at-risk individuals for clinical follow-up.

No medical claim: SAMVEDNA is a screening aid for demonstration, not a diagnosis. Clinical assessment must follow standard care.

RESEARCH FOUNDATION

Acoustic sensing for knee health — peer-reviewed basis

Our approach builds on published work that studies acoustic emissions from the knee during sit-to-stand-sit movements. The paper reports measurable differences in acoustic features between healthy and OA groups — the hierarchy we mirror in the demo model.

REFERENCE
Verma D.K. et al. (2026), “Knee joint health assessment using acoustic sensors in osteoarthritis: a quantitative and parametric study”, Med. Eng. Phys. 147, 015006.
H1/H2/H3 vs OA — acoustic energy, duration, hits and amplitude show stepwise separation; used as baseline hierarchy for demo-rule-v1 only.
● Clearly labelled demo model • Not clinically validated → replace before real use

Illustrative — see paper for exact numbers and methods.
